**GPT Snippets** helps you collect, search, and reuse the best parts of ChatGPT responses — without saving entire conversations.

If you regularly use ChatGPT for work, study, or writing, useful answers can quickly get lost in long chats. GPT Snippets lets you extract only the most valuable snippets and keep them in a searchable local collection.

### What you can do with GPT Snippets:
- Save selected snippets from ChatGPT conversations
- Click a snippet to navigate to its source
- Search through your saved snippets instantly
- Reuse snippets whenever you need them
- Copy snippets as clean Markdown for tools like Notion, Obsidian, or GitHub
- Keep everything stored locally in your browser (NO transmission to any server!)
- Export your collection to MD or JSON so that the data is truly yours

GPT Snippets is designed for people who want to keep **only what matters** — not full answers, not entire chats, just the best parts worth saving.

Perfect for developers, writers, researchers, and anyone who uses ChatGPT as a daily thinking or productivity tool.
